# Food sources and lodging around IIM Calcutta, Joka

**Researched:** 2026-11-15. **Status:** public-listing research only — nobody has been contacted, and
nothing here is a partner, a supplier, a signed organisation or a promise.

This file exists so that a Joka pilot has a starting list instead of a guess. Every row came from a
public aggregator (Zomato, Justdial, District, Weddingz/WeddingWire, Goibibo/MakeMyTrip) reached by
search on the date above. Aggregator data goes stale, addresses move and kitchens close, so treat
each row as a lead to verify, never as a fact about a business.

## Why these categories

Surplus food does not appear evenly. It follows kitchens that cook in bulk against a schedule:

| Category | Typical surplus | Timing | Fit for this product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banquet and event venues | Large, in a single window | Late evening, after a function ends | Best fit: volume, one pickup, predictable end time |
| Hotel kitchens with breakfast/buffet | Medium, daily | Late morning, after service | Good fit if the kitchen will hold food safely until a window |
| Restaurants and eateries | Small, unpredictable | Closing time | Low value per pickup; only worth it at cluster density |
| Campus institutional kitchens (IIMC mess/MDC) | Large, daily | Fixed service times | High value, but needs the institute's own approval |

## Getting there and what the map says

- The campus gate is on Diamond Harbour Road (NH-12) at Joka, Kolkata 700104. Joka is the southern
  terminus of the Kolkata Metro Purple Line, which is why the budget hotels advertise "walk to Joka
  metro".
- Thakurpukur (3A bus stand, Thakurpukur Bazar, cancer hospital) is the next commercial cluster
  north-east of Joka and is where most of the mid-range hotels and banquet halls sit.
- Behala, Barisha and Pailan are further out but still on the same road. Pailan and Kriparampur hold
  the large resort and farm venues.
- Distances above are approximate bands taken from booking pages ("2.8 km drive to IIM Calcutta" and
  similar), not measurements. The product's own transit estimate is the thing to trust once an
  organisation with coordinates exists.

## What has to happen before any of these become a Generator

None of this is a partnership, and a phone number on a listings site is not consent to be contacted
about a food-rescue scheme. In order:

1. **Pick a shortlist by likelihood, not by rating.** Banquet venues with in-house catering first,
   hotel kitchens second, eateries only as a cluster.
2. **Find the decision-maker through a legitimate channel** — a published business contact, a
   referral, or a visit. Do not scrape personal numbers and do not bulk-call a listing directory.
3. **Confirm the regulated facts** on a call or visit: legal name, FSSAI licence number and its
   expiry, who signs for the kitchen, what they already do with surplus, and whether they will hold
   food at a safe temperature until a pickup window.
4. **Record them as an organisation** through the normal onboarding flow, with evidence uploaded to
   private document storage. The public page must keep saying "not a partner" until a human has
   verified them, and `/near-iim-calcutta` must not imply otherwise.
5. **Re-verify this file** before use: every row is dated 2026-11-15 and none of it was confirmed by
   a phone call, a visit or an FSSAI lookup.
