# Human launch and safety checklist

This is an **unapproved checklist**, not legal advice, a food-safety standard, a partnership agreement or authorization to operate. The application records participants' declarations; it cannot independently determine food safety, identity, arrival, or policy compliance. Keep `OPERATIONS_ENABLED=false` and use only fictional data until accountable humans complete the applicable reviews and explicitly authorize a limited pilot.

## Decisions and approvals required

- [ ] Identify an accountable operator, the service area, incident contacts and operating hours; obtain explicit written authorization for a controlled pilot and the participating Generator and Receiver organisations. Verify their consent, identities, authority, capabilities, applicable registrations and contact information. No directory listing implies a partnership.
- [ ] A qualified food-safety professional and legal counsel must review applicable current laws and approve documented, locality-specific procedures before any real food is handled. Review FSSAI's [surplus-food recovery and distribution regulations](https://nflgaziabad.fssai.gov.in/food-safety-and-standards-regulations/) and [Indian Food Sharing Alliance resources](https://sharefood.fssai.gov.in/) as starting points; verify the applicable gazette notifications, amendments and licensing obligations. No blanket expiry times, transport temperatures or eligibility thresholds are endorsed by this repository.
- [ ] Approve a specific policy version, training materials, allergen and labeling procedures, temperature/handling controls, pickup windows, acceptance and refusal criteria, custody, disposal, recalls, incident escalation and recordkeeping. Exercise rejection and unsafe-food procedures offline with qualified staff. The current incident hold is not a complete recall or refusal workflow.
- [ ] Obtain legal/privacy review for the data collected, lawful basis and notices, retention/deletion schedules, subject requests, vendor contracts, international transfers, breach response and protection of minors where relevant. Review the [DPDP Act commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) and [DPDP Rules 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), including their phased commencement and any updates, with counsel; do not assume every provision starts on the same date.
- [ ] Confirm cyber incident reporting obligations and a staffed response process with counsel. CERT-In's [FAQ on its 2022 directions](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf) describes reporting applicable incidents within six hours of notice and providing further information later; determine scope and current requirements for the operator.

## Engineering and operations gates

- [ ] Execute CI on the actual hosting platform and investigate all failures; test Compose, image lifecycle and Windows setup on supported hosts. Exercise authenticated role/tenant isolation and every privileged, decline/refusal, hold/recall, expiry and worker-recovery path against real PostgreSQL with adversarial inputs and independent security review. Re-run the browser, `test:local-auth`, `test:auth-security`, and `test:auth-roles` suites on the exact deployment commit; local synthetic results are not production validation.
- [ ] Owner only: enter the PostgreSQL connection URLs, `AUTH_SECRET`, three encryption/HMAC keys, `APP_URL`, and a real transactional SMTP sender/credential in the host's secure environment settings. Never place those values in GitHub, `.env.example`, chat, or a production env file committed to the repository. Follow `AUTHENTICATION_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md` for the exact names and post-deployment tests.
- [ ] Validate SMTP deliverability and retries with an authorized transactional provider; configure monitoring, alerting, log redaction and on-call ownership. Test backup, off-host storage, restore, migration rollback/recovery, and disaster response without exposing personal data. Configure TLS, host hardening, secret rotation and access management.
- [ ] Implement and validate private storage, malware scanning and access/retention controls before accepting uploads. Confirm third-party API terms and attribution before expanding public discovery. Avoid using the local SMTP sink or test fixture roles in any shared environment.
- [ ] Have authorized operations and safety approvers sign off on policy, verified partners, training and evidence for the exact environment. Explicitly authorize enablement only after all gates, including unresolved ones in `BUILD_STATUS.md`, are closed. Document rollback and immediate pause procedures.
