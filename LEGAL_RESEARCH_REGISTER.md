# Legal research register

**Status: NOT VERIFIED. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** No provision in this file has been checked against a
primary source by a qualified professional, and no legal applicability decision has been made. It
exists so the owner knows exactly what must be verified, by whom, before real operations.

The application must not present any of this as compliance. `FOOD_SAFETY_CONTROLS.md` says the same
thing in product terms.

## How to use this register

For each row: check the current text on the official source, record the exact provision, the date
checked, the reviewer, and the action required. A row is only "done" when a named reviewer has signed
it. Engineering cannot close these rows.

## What the software already supports

- Recording partner evidence and a verification decision with a reason.
- Marking policy and privacy pages as drafts, and refusing to present them as approved.
- Collecting terms acceptance at registration.
- Keeping an append-only record of decisions, so a later review can reconstruct what was decided and
  by whom.

## What the software deliberately does not do

It does not decide that a partner is legally entitled to operate, does not assert compliance in any
interface, and does not substitute an uploaded document for a professional check. A verified badge
means "a human reviewed the evidence you supplied", nothing more, and the interface says so.
