From offer to receipt

Every handover has a next step.

Clear details and human decisions at each point in the chain.

  1. 01 / OFFER

    A donor lists what is available

    Food details, quantities, ingredients, known allergens, timing and handling instructions are recorded as human declarations.

  2. 02 / REVIEW

    A Receiver reviews the offer

    Only an eligible, verified organisation can review a matching offer and decide whether it can receive it.

  3. 03 / HANDOVER

    A Helper completes the handover

    An approved Helper records collection. The Receiver confirms arrival directly, creating the delivery receipt.

Trust is part of the workflow

Clear records. Human decisions.

Food redistribution needs more than a matching button. Annapurna Link keeps declarations, handovers and exceptions visible to the people responsible for them.

01

Verified means human-reviewed

Account and organisation requests stay pending until an authorised person records a decision.

02

Traceable, not exaggerated

Only confirmed deliveries count. Units are not converted into meal estimates.

03

Safety stays a human responsibility

The platform records declarations and events. It does not inspect, certify or guarantee food safety.
